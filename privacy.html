Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Sparkly AI ("the App") is a business dashboard that lets a seller ("you") monitor and manage AI-assisted sales conversations with their customers across messaging platforms such as WhatsApp. This policy explains what information the App collects, why, and how it's used.

Information we collect

To provide the App's core functionality, we collect and store:

  • Seller account information — your chat/login ID and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in readable form).
  • Business information you provide — product details, pricing, delivery policies, and other business rules you set up through the App or its AI assistant.
  • Customer conversation data — messages exchanged between your customers and your AI sales assistant, including customer phone numbers, names, and order details, so you can review and manage these conversations.
  • Usage data — basic technical information (such as device type) needed to operate the App reliably.

How we use this information

Information is used solely to operate the App: displaying your conversations, letting your AI assistant respond to customers according to the rules you set, and letting you manage your business data. We do not sell your data or your customers' data to third parties.

Third-party services

The App relies on the following third-party services to function:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business API) — to send and receive customer messages.
  • Supabase — to securely store your account and conversation data.
  • Anthropic — to power the AI assistant that drafts and sends replies.
  • Telegram — optionally, for account notifications and management commands.

Each of these providers processes data under their own privacy policies.

Data retention

Your business and conversation data is retained for as long as your account is active, so you can continue reviewing past conversations and order history. You may request deletion at any time (see below).

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and associated data at any time. To do so, use the "Delete my account" option in the App's Settings, or contact us at rawanduski135@gmail.com.

Data security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all data in transit and securely hashed passwords. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information using commercially reasonable safeguards.

Children's privacy

The App is a business tool intended for adult sellers and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to rawanduski135@gmail.com.
